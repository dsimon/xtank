new epoch, for 20 resets over 60 min. Four of those cycles exercise a
mid-epoch admin departure (2 s removal + admin transfer) and a **fresh**
rejoin, proving connection reuse. Progress is written incrementally to the
evidence dir so the soak survives an agent-session death.
