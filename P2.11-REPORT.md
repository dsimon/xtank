## Goal

A bounded, authorized, read-only client model for the protocol-v1
`VIEW_SNAPSHOT` stream: epoch/lifetime handling, a monotonic render timeline and
the approved D2-E policy — 15 Hz snapshots, one-tick (66.7 ms) interpolation
buffer, 60 FPS render target, no extrapolation and no local prediction — with a
camera that follows the controlled player's displayed pose at the fixed legacy
logical viewport/world scale.

## Design

- **Bounded ring.** `XtkClientModel` holds `XTK_SNAPSHOT_RING = 8` full
  snapshots per epoch in ascending `server_tick` order. Feeding a newer snapshot
  evicts the oldest; the model is heap-sized (~hundreds of KiB) and documented as
  such.
- **Epoch / lifetime.** A newer epoch clears ring, pose and timeline and snaps
  (D2-E, VIEW-v1 §5.4). A stale epoch returns `STALE_EPOCH`; a non-newer
  `snapshot_sequence` or `server_tick` returns `DUPLICATE`. Interpolation only
  matches entities by `(kind, id, generation)` — vehicles by slot+generation,
  bullets by id+owner slot+owner generation, explosions by id.
- **Interpolation.** The frame is built from the **older** bracketing snapshot
  (the discrete base: armor/ammo/walls/landmarks/map/radar/self are held, never
  blended) with only positions and shortest-arc headings of lifetime-matched
  dynamic entities interpolated by the one-tick-buffered render fraction. An
  entity present only in the older view is dropped (never a stale ghost); one
  present only in the newer view is shown snapped.
- **Timeline.** `timeline_estimate(now)` is monotonic and anchored on the newest
  received tick; render tick = `estimate − 1.0`. Render before the oldest clamps
  to `BUFFERING`; render at/after the newest clamps to `UNDERRUN` and freezes.
- **Snap policy.** A lifetime-matched vehicle that moves more than
  `XTK_MODEL_TELEPORT_DISTANCE = 256` world units between two snapshots is
  snapped, not slid (there is no wire teleport flag; this is a client
  discontinuity heuristic). Death/respawn does not match across generations, so
  the old life disappears and the new one appears; the camera freezes the last
  displayed own pose (`VIEW-v1.md` §5.4) until the new life is rendered.
- **Camera.** Centred on the controlled player's interpolated (displayed) pose;
  viewport (768) and world scale (1:1) are invariant under interpolation and
  frame rate. On underrun/disconnect it freezes.

## Constraints honored

- **No local simulation.** The model/timeline/camera contain no movement,
  damage, RNG or control code; `timeline.sh` statically rejects X/SDL/simulation
  references and undefined engine symbols. Snapshot fields never feed back into
  physics.
- **No arbitrary camera.** The camera is always centred on the own displayed
  pose at the frozen viewport; it cannot request a region beyond the authorized
  own-pose window.
- **Presentation never schedules ticks.** Tick consumption is driven by wall
  time; the 30/60/144 FPS runs feed the same ticks and render the same pose.
- **D2-E exactly.** 15 Hz snapshots, one-tick buffer, no extrapolation, no
  prediction; only matching authorized IDs/generations are interpolated;
  discrete armor/ammo/walls/scores/results are held, not blended; history clears
  on epoch/reset; pose freezes on underrun with a connection status.
- Evidence under `/tmp/opencode/xtank-phase2/p2.11/` (`timeline/`, `build/`,
  `build-win/`, `oracle-before/`, `oracle-after/`, `phase1-check.log`). Not in
  Git; no generated files in the checkout.

## Deferred / notes

- **Wiring** of the model into the live client frame loop is P2.14 (input is
  P2.12, HUD/map/radar P2.13); `main.c` is unchanged here.
- **Teleport snap** is a client discontinuity heuristic (`>256` world units per
  matched tick); if a future protocol revision adds an explicit
  discontinuity/keyframe entity flag, the model should use it instead.
- **Events** (`DEATH`/`RESPAWN`) are not consumed by the model: the snapshot's
  lifecycle flags/generation already carry the authoritative truth the model
  needs, per the plan's "snapshots carry lifecycle/score truth so lost cosmetic
  events cannot leave stale entities".
- **Explosion animation frame** remains outside the `VIEW_SNAPSHOT` DTO (P2.10
  note); the renderer draws the object's last frame until the snapshot carries
  the life/frame or an event supplies it.
